30B is not remotely feasible on this hardware. Even DiffusionGemma (26B MoE, 4B active) needed TPU v5p pods — Google's custom hardware. On L40S nodes, you'd need:

  • A cluster you do not have (16+ GPUs)
  • Network interconnects you do not have (InfiniBand or NVLink — 100 Gbps Ethernet is too slow for 30B FSDP)
  • Months you do not have (160 days minimum)
  • A budget you do not have (R$ 500K+)

The 30B question answers itself: focus on 300M → 1B → maybe 3B.
