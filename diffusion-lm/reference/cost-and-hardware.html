Diffusion vs Autoregressive: Key Differences

DimensionAutoregressiveDiffusion
Training FLOPs (to same loss)1× (baseline)16× (per SMDM)
Inference FLOPs (per sequence)seq_len × cost_per_passsteps × cost_per_pass
Inference parallelismSequential (token-by-token)Parallel (all tokens each step)
KV-cache needed?Yes (O(seq_len × d))No
Sampling speed (1.1B model)1× (baseline)1.4×
Compute-quality tradeoff at inferenceNone (fixed cost)Tunable (more steps = better)
Data efficiencyHigherLower (needs more tokens)
